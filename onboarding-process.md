# ATP Student Onboarding Process

## ONBOARDING BY TIER

**Gold:** Mandatory Group Orientation every Monday 4:00 PM EST (Zoom).
CSM walks through program structure, expectations, resources, first 30-day plan.

**Platinum & Diamond:** Private 1:1 Onboarding Call (30–45 min via Calendly).
CSM must confirm onboarding sheets are COMPLETED before the call.
Review sheets in detail, identify experience level, customize action plan.

**First 24–72 hours are critical.**
Set the student's confidence, clarity, and perception of the program.
Delays or confusion here = elevated refund risk.

- Onboarding emails sent automatically from success@abuvthepar.com
- CSM must confirm: sent, no bounce-back, student acknowledges within 24 hours
- CSAs create Discord 1:1 chats for Platinum and Diamond students

## DOWNGRADE PROCESS

Never process immediately. Always follow these steps:

1. **Probe** — what's the real cause? (Most are financial, not value-driven.)
2. **Mandatory 1:1 call** — required before any downgrade is approved
3. **Explore alternatives** — OPM/funding options, value re-alignment, short-term deferment
4. **Escalate** if high-value student → Lead CSM before final approval
5. **Execute** — DocuSign updated agreement → student signs before refund → update CRM → notify Finance
6. **Follow up within 14 days**

**Guardrails:**
- No downgrades over email
- Never lead with refund details
- Never promise financial adjustments without internal confirmation
- Never mention tier downgrade to a student unless Brittany has approved it for that specific case

## REFUND CALL FRAMEWORK

Handle with confidence, empathy, and a path to retain.

1. Mindset & Preparation — don't take it personally; this is a save opportunity
2. Call Flow & Agenda — acknowledge → explore root cause → present solutions
3. Objections & Responses — use A.E.A.C.
4. Solutions Menu — pause, defer, downgrade, accountability partner, new CSM
5. Closing Scripts & Follow-Up — document everything, follow up within 72hrs
