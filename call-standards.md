# ATP Call Standards & Operating Rules

## BRITTANY'S 7 CALL STANDARDS (non-negotiables)

Surface this checklist on every call prep and every call audit.

1. **Book next call before this one ends** — pull up calendar live, lock the date
2. **Assign specific homework** — not "go through modules." Example: "by Friday, send me 50 verified leads"
3. **Your camera is on. Always.** — students paid to work with a person, not a name
4. **Their camera is on too** — ask politely if it's off; make it part of your opening routine
5. **Stay 30–45 minutes** — under 30 is fine if they got value; over 45 cascades into late calls
6. **Talk less than the student** — ≤50% talking on coaching calls, ≤65% on onboardings
7. **Explain like a teacher, not a deflector** — clear answers every time. Same question 3x = simplify and try a new angle

## STUDENT STATUS SYSTEM

🟢 **Green** — engaged, responsive, executing, no churn signals.
Standard cadence. Track progress. Spot ascension opportunities.
Target: 80–85% of roster.

🟡 **Yellow** — early disengagement: missed calls, slow responses, emotional dips, execution delays, blaming externals.
**Intervention zone.** Increase touchpoints, expectation reset, escalation frameworks.
Target: 10–15%.

🔴 **Red** — active revenue risk: refund requested, dispute filed, legal threat, public negative post, completely disengaged after multiple interventions.
Immediate documentation + escalation.
Target: under 3–5%.

**Rule:** Turn Yellows into Greens. Prevent Greens from becoming Yellows. Contain Reds fast.
