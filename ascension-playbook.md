# ATP Ascension Playbook

## WHAT AN ASCENSION IS

Student moves from current tier to a higher one because they've outgrown what they have.

**The only reason to do it:** student needs more to succeed.
**What it is NOT:** a sale, a pitch, a quota, a one-shot moment, for every student.
**CSM role:** Recognize, Explore, Surface — the student decides, not you.

## THE TRUST FOUNDATION

"You cannot ascend a student who doesn't trust you."

Trust is built BEFORE the ascension conversation — on every call, every fast reply, every honest answer, every commitment kept.

**Never ascend students who are:** in financial hardship, mid-crisis, recent setbacks, or brand-new enrollments.

## R.E.A.D.Y. FRAMEWORK

### R — Recognize (before the conversation)
3-question test — stop if any answer is no:
1. Has this student gotten value from their current tier?
2. Do I see specific signals (not gut feel) that they want more?
3. Would I recommend this upgrade with zero commission attached?

**Signals to look for:** consistent engagement, completing milestones, "what's next" / "I want to scale" / "I'm ready for more", hitting outcomes their tier was designed to deliver.

### E — Explore (most important, most skipped)
- "What's been your biggest unlock so far?"
- "What are you trying to build over the next 6 months?"
- "What feels like the gap between where you are and where you want to be?"
- "What does success look like for you a year from now?"
- "What kind of support do you wish you had right now?"
- "What would have to be true for you to feel like you're crushing it?"

Listen for: the gap, the emotion, the fit.

### A — Align
Template: "Based on what you said about [their goal/gap], it sounds like [observation]. The reason I'm bringing this up is [tier] is specifically built for that — here's how..."

Don't: list every feature, compare tiers upfront, talk price yet, oversell.

### D — Discuss
1. Frame clearly: "Here's what I'd recommend: moving you to Platinum."
2. Lay out 3–5 specifics most relevant to THIS student + price + start + transition
3. Ask "What questions do you have about how this would work?"
4. Handle objections with A.E.A.C.

Don't: manufacture urgency, compare dismissively to lower-tier students, promise specific results.

### Y — Yield
Hand the decision to the student cleanly:
- "What feels right to you?"
- "Where does that leave you?"
- "How does that sit with you?"
- "What do you want to do?"

**Timing on a 30–45 min call:**
- 0–5: Check-in
- 5–20: Coaching content
- 20–28: Explore ("Can I shift gears for a minute?")
- 28–32: Align
- 32–40: Discuss
- 40–45: Yield

## A.E.A.C. OBJECTION HANDLING

**A — Acknowledge** (make them feel heard FIRST)
- "That makes sense — it's a real consideration."
- "I hear you. That's a fair thing to think about."
- NEVER: "Yeah, but..." / "I understand, however..."

**E — Explore** (find the REAL objection)
- "Can you tell me a little more about what's coming up for you?"
- "Help me understand — is it the dollar amount, the timing, or something else?"
- "What would have to be true for the price to feel right?"

**A — Address** (respond to what they ACTUALLY said)
- One clean response lands harder than three desperate ones
- Be honest even when inconvenient

**C — Confirm** (check it landed)
- "Does that address what you were asking?"
- "How does that sit with you?"
- "Is that the answer you were looking for, or is there more to it?"

## 12 MOST COMMON OBJECTIONS

1. **"It's too expensive"** → Don't defend price. Find what "too expensive" means — value clarity or cash flow?
2. **"I need to think about it"** → Probe gently. Set specific follow-up date: "Why don't I check back Tuesday?"
3. **"I want to talk to my spouse/partner"** → Arm them with answers for their partner. Offer a 3-way call. Lock follow-up date.
4. **"I want to see results first"** → Be willing to agree they're not ready. If close to a milestone the upgrade accelerates, surface gently.
5. **"Can I get a discount?"** → Never discount as closing tactic. Lead with payment plan: "We don't discount because the value doesn't change."
6. **"What if it doesn't work for me?"** → Fear of failure. "I can't guarantee results — no honest program can."
7. **"I just signed up — why are you upselling me?"** → Take seriously. Offer to back off entirely.
8. **"I had a bad experience with another program"** → Meet with patience. Demonstrate, don't promise.
9. **"Can you guarantee results?"** → "Here's what I can guarantee: what we provide. I can't guarantee your outcome — that depends on you."
10. **"It's just not the right time"** → Honor it with specific follow-up OR probe: "Sometimes 'not the right time' means 'not interested.' Which is closer?"
11. **"I want to do my own research first"** → Support it. Gently surface: "Sometimes that means 'I'm not ready to decide.' Is that more accurate?"
12. **"What happens if I want to cancel?"** → Buying signal. Answer clearly — no hedging. Clarity builds trust.

**Universal principles:**
- 3-second pause before responding signals confidence
- Get curious before persuasive
- Say the hard thing if it's true
- Don't stack responses
- Know when to walk away — don't push past 3+ legitimate objections

## OPENING THE ASCENSION CONVERSATION

**Best timing:** Month 4–6.

**5-Beat Opening:**
1. Close out coaching cleanly — "Your action items are X, Y, Z. Any other questions?"
2. Signal shift, ask permission — "Can I shift gears for a few minutes? I've been thinking about your trajectory — is now okay?"
3. Reflect back the journey — name actual milestones, use their words
4. Name the inflection — "You've proven the model works... but what got you to $2K isn't what gets you to $10K. Does that match?"
5. Open the door — "I want to talk about what comes next. Can I ask you a few questions?"

NEVER: "So actually, I wanted to talk to you about an upgrade..."

## FOLLOW-UP CADENCE

70% of ascensions close on the 2nd, 3rd, or 4th touchpoint — NOT same-call.

| Outcome | Active Window | Cadence |
|---------|--------------|---------|
| Soft Yes | 7 days | Same-day, 24–48hr, Day 5, Day 7 |
| Genuine Maybe | 14 days | Same-day, Day 3, Day 7, Day 14 |
| Soft No | 24 hours | Same-day thank-you only |

**Hard No = honor it. Never follow up on ascension again. Ever.**

**Reopen only on a signal:** new revenue milestone, verbal ("I want to scale"), behavioral (doing higher-tier work), AND minimum 60–90 days have passed.
