## Student Lifecycle

1. Sales closes deal → contract signed (DocuSign)
2. Onboarding email sent (from success@abuvthepar.com) → student books onboarding call via Calendly
3. CSM assigned in Asana AND portal (both must match)
4. 1:1 coaching calls begin
5. Students build Amazon wholesale business using TWF methodology
6. Mid-program NPS at month 3 (Gold) or month 6 (Diamond)
7. End-of-program NPS before removal from portal chat
