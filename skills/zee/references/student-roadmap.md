# EEC Student Roadmap — The 30-Day Sprint (31 Tasks)

Use this to instantly know where any student is and what to coach next.
A student stuck on Phase 1 after 30 days is at-risk.

---

## PHASE 1: ONBOARDING (Days 1–7) — Business & Amazon Setup
**Goal:** Get legally established and Amazon account active

1. Watch Course Overview & Set Your Goals
2. Research & Start LLC Formation (Prime Corporate Services)
3. Apply for EIN (Employer Identification Number)
4. Set Up Business Email & Banking
5. Obtain Resale Certificate
6. Create Amazon Seller Central Account (Part 1)
7. Complete Seller Central Application (Part 2)
8. Install Essential Tools (SmartScout, Keepa, Loom)

**Common stalls here:** LLC/EIN paperwork delays, Seller Central verification loops

---

## PHASE 2: LEARNING & MARKET RESEARCH (Days 8–17)
**Goal:** Master the wholesale model and build 100+ brand lead list

9. Complete Module 2: Amazon Marketplace Basics
10. Master SmartScout for Brand Discovery
11. Learn Product Research Fundamentals
12. Set Up Lead List Spreadsheet
13. Learn Lead Generation Strategies
14. Build Lead List: Add 20 Brands (20 total)
15. Build Lead List: Add 20 Brands (40 total)
16. Build Lead List: Add 20 Brands (60 total)
17. Build Lead List: Add 20 Brands (80 total)
🏁 **CHECKPOINT:** Build Lead List: 100 Brands Total!

**Common stalls here:** SmartScout overwhelm (→ M3 first), 100-brand list fear (→ "get to 20 first")

---

## PHASE 3: OUTREACH & ACCOUNT OPENING (Days 18–30)
**Goal:** Open 5+ supplier/brand accounts and prepare for first order

18. Set Up Gmass & Prepare Outreach Templates
19. Send First 50 Outreach Emails via Gmass
20. Send Second 50 Outreach Emails (100 total)
21. Review Responses & Reply to Interested Brands
22. Complete Wholesale Applications (5+ Brands)
23. Follow Up on Pending Applications
24. Continue Applications & Follow-Ups
25. Request Pricelists from Approved Suppliers
26. Analyze Pricelists & Select Products
27. Finalize Account Details with Suppliers
28. Confirm Payment Method / Place First Order
29. Place First Order (test order)
30-31. Final ordering steps & confirmation

**Common stalls here:** First email fear (→ "50 emails, expect 5 responses"), first PO fear (→ test order first)

## PORTAL TOOLS TO CHECK FOR EACH STUDENT

- **My Roadmap** — shows % completion and which tasks are done
- **Brand Leads CRM** — pipeline of brand outreach (Not Yet Contacted → Approved)
  - A student with 0 leads is at ground zero
  - A student stuck at "Email Sent" for 2+ weeks needs outreach coaching
- **CSM Panel** — student health data, last login, assigned modules
