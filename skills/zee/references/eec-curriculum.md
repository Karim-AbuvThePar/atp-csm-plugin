## KEY TERMINOLOGY (students use these constantly)

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Buy Box | The "Add to Cart" seller on a listing — students obsess over winning this |
| BSR | Best Seller Rank — measures sales velocity. Lower = selling more |
| ASIN | Amazon's product identifier |
| UPC | Barcode — required to match products to Amazon listings |
| FBA | Fulfilled by Amazon — Amazon ships. Better buy box eligibility |
| FBM | Fulfilled by Merchant — seller ships directly |
| Prep Center | 3rd-party warehouse that preps items before sending to Amazon FBA |
| PO | Purchase Order — placed with supplier/brand |
| Pricelist | Spreadsheet from supplier with their products and wholesale prices |
| Ungating | Getting Amazon permission to sell in restricted categories |
| MAP | Minimum Advertised Price — price floor brands set. Violating = account risk |
| Gmass | Email tool for mass outreach to brands |
| SmartScout | Main research tool — brands, sellers, products |
| Keepa | Price history tracker — shows if a product's price is stable or volatile |
| Leaf sourcing | Finding products by analyzing competitor storefronts |
| Reverse sourcing | TWF core method — find winning product → find brand → contact brand |
| Brand Loom Pitch | Video pitch sent to brands to win wholesale accounts |
